## INSTAGRAM
Five flat loaves? Your starter's fine. Your fridge isn't. 👇

Six years of baking, 400 people taught, one mistake I see every single week:

1. Proof on the counter first, not in the fridge.
2. Poke it. Slow spring, stops halfway = ready.
3. Then the fridge. It holds the dough; it doesn't proof it.

Skip step 2 and the fridge locks in a loaf that was never ready.
