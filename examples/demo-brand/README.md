# Demo brand: Crumb & Co

A complete, fictional example so you can see what the studio produces before you run
`/setup`. Sana runs a small bakery and teaches home bakers. Nothing here is real; the
baking claims are common knowledge and are marked as such in `resources.md`, which is
exactly how your own unverified claims should look.

- `BRAND.md` — the filled profile the interview produced
- `output/2026-09-12-flat-sourdough/` — one reel package: brief, script, sources,
  captions, and the three gate reports

Read `voiceover.md` out loud with a timer. It is budgeted for 133 words per minute; if
you are faster or slower, that is the whole reason `/setup` times you.
